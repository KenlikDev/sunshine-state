# Source Register

## Purpose
Record public references used for research. Sources inform high-level design analysis only; they are not proof of undocumented features and do not authorize reuse of proprietary content.

## Source categories
- Official publisher materials
- Reputable journalism and attributed reporting
- Publicly available community analysis
- Engine and tool documentation
- User-generated speculation (low confidence)

## Leak handling policy
Do not download, store, or use leaked source code, private credentials, confidential internal documents, or ripped proprietary assets. Publicly reported leaks may be catalogued as historical reporting. Mark each claim as verified, reported, disputed, or unverified. Never turn a rumour into an MVP requirement without an independent design justification.
